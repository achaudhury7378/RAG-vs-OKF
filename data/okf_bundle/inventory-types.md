title: Why inventory exists and the cost trade-off
description: Ordering, holding and shortage costs and the five types of stock
---
Every inventory model trades off three costs: ordering (S per order), holding (h = i x c per unit per year, where i is the carrying rate) and shortage. Be ready to derive [EOQ](eoq.md) and the [newsvendor](newsvendor.md) on a whiteboard.

| Type | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Cycle stock | Ordering in batches (Q/2 on average) |
| Safety stock | Buffer against demand and lead-time uncertainty, see [safety stock](safety-stock-and-rop.md) |
| Pipeline stock | In transit; average = d-bar x L (Little's law) |
| Anticipation stock | Pre-build for seasons or promotions |
| Decoupling stock | Lets stages run independently |
