title: SCOR model and core supply chain terms
description: Plan-Source-Make-Deliver-Return framing and the vocabulary interviewers expect
---
The classic SCOR model splits the chain into Plan, Source, Make, Deliver, Return (plus Enable). The newer SCOR Digital Standard renamed some of these; verify the current labels if you cite it by name.

Useful terms: FTL vs LTL, cross-dock (no storage, direct transfer), 3PL, inbound vs outbound logistics, MTS / MTO / ATO / ETO (make-to-stock, make-to-order, assemble-to-order, engineer-to-order), push vs pull, and the customer order decoupling point (where forecast-driven push turns into order-driven pull).

Interviewers use this to check you speak the planner's language. Tie every model you mention to a metric, see [supply chain metrics](supply-chain-metrics.md). Decisions by function are in the [planning hierarchy](planning-hierarchy.md).
